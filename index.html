    Dear Shizuka,

    Happy birthday, kiddo. I wanted to make you something that feels a little like the way I see you: soft around the edges, full of light, and impossible to forget. So take a breath, settle in, and let me say a few things.

    Shizuka, I know I have not always been the best at showing it. Sometimes I get distant, sometimes I do not find the right words, but none of that changes how much you mean to me. You have become one of the rare people who make the world feel less ordinary. If life ever takes us in different directions, I hope you still remember that somewhere, in some quiet corner of my heart, I will always be grateful our paths crossed. You are one of a kind. Truly.

    I hope this next chapter is gentle with you, generous to you, and filled with the kind of happiness that stays long after the moment has passed.

    The details I never want to forget

    The little things that feel like you.

    It is never just one thing. It is the whole constellation.

    Your unmistakable energy

    You have this way of making even the most random moments feel alive. Your goofy jokes, your chaotic energy, the way you are completely yourself — it all feels so naturally you. I could search through a hundred different versions of the world and still not find another person quite like you.

    The way you stayed

    Nearly a year of conversations, little check-ins, and simply being there. That means more to me than I probably manage to explain. People come and go, but you gave me a kind of consistency I had not known before. I do not take that for granted, Shizu.

    Our little moments

    Those long conversations that somehow turned into hours. The gossip, the laughter, the ridiculous topics, the tiny things only we would understand. I miss that feeling sometimes — like the rest of the world had gone quiet and it was just us talking. Those memories still glow.

    Simply being you

    There is no grand explanation for this one. I am just glad you exist, and even more glad that our lives happened to overlap. You make ordinary days feel brighter without even trying. If I could keep one thing from all these passing moments, it would be the fact that I got to know you.

    A wish for every version of tomorrow

    To you, across every season.

    November 13, 2026 — and every day that comes after. Wherever life places us, I hope it gives you beautiful reasons to smile. I hope your world keeps opening into new skies, new places, and new versions of happiness. And even when a moment becomes a memory, I hope it remains a warm one.

    Happy birthday, my favorite person. May you find people who cherish you, chances that excite you, and peace that feels like coming home. I am always rooting for you. With so much love, — Aruu.

    May your light always find its way home.

    With love, Aruu ♡
